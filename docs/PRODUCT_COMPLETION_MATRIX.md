## Evidence rules

`L3` claims require the compiled-Qt build record; `L5` requires real Mint session behavior and is scoped to the exact ELF hash. A `MINT VERIFIED` direct self-test does not cover the smoke runner, visual approval, window-manager/session behavior, release acceptance or a different binary. Record `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT RUN`, `NOT APPLICABLE`, `SKIPPED`, `SYNTHETIC`, `CI-SCOPED`, `RUNTIME VERIFIED`, `MINT VERIFIED`, `NOT_PROVEN` or `IN REVIEW` exactly. Do not convert a unit suite, mock, screenshot, green CI check, draft PR, planner or build-condition failure into product acceptance. No release readiness and no owner visual acceptance is claimed anywhere in this matrix. Preserve PR holds, protected visuals and artifact ownership boundaries.
