## Evidence rules

`L3` claims now require the compiled-Qt build record; `L5` requires real Mint behavior and is scoped to the exact ELF hash. The direct self-test `MINT VERIFIED` result does not cover the smoke runner, visual approval, window-manager/session behavior, release acceptance or a different binary. Record `PASS`, `FAIL`, `BLOCKED`, `NOT RUN`, `NOT APPLICABLE`, `SKIPPED`, `SYNTHETIC`, `CI-SCOPED`, `RUNTIME VERIFIED`, `MINT VERIFIED`, or `NOT_PROVEN` exactly. Do not convert a unit suite, mock, screenshot, green CI check or build-condition failure into product acceptance. Preserve PR holds and protected visuals.
